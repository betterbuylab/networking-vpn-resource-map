# Buyer-fit checklist

- [ ] Are dead zones caused by layout, ISP gear, or both?
- [ ] Can you run Ethernet backhaul between mesh nodes?
- [ ] Is the pain point Wi-Fi coverage or VPN confusion on streaming devices?
- [ ] Do many household devices compete on the same 2.4 GHz band?
- [ ] Is double-NAT from ISP modem/router combo affecting stability?
- [ ] Do you need VPN on a router versus per-device apps?
- [ ] Is privacy paperwork more important than streaming convenience?
- [ ] Will a budget VPN meet your device count and refund expectations?
