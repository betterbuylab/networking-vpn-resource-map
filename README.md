## Buyer-fit questions

1. Are dead zones caused by layout, ISP gear, or both?
2. Can you run Ethernet backhaul between mesh nodes?
3. Is the pain point Wi-Fi coverage or VPN confusion on streaming devices?
4. Do many household devices compete on the same 2.4 GHz band?
5. Is double-NAT from ISP modem/router combo affecting stability?
6. Do you need VPN on a router versus per-device apps?
7. Is privacy paperwork more important than streaming convenience?
8. Will a budget VPN meet your device count and refund expectations?
